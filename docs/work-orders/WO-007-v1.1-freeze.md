## Known limit (for the record)
If IO1 still goes high at power-up, the glitch happens in the Optiboot bootloader before `setup()` runs, when the pin is still an input. Only a pull-down resistor on IO1 fixes that. Not soldering before travel. The GCC baseline script flags and excludes the first session after each power-up.

## Bench check (Chip, after 31 Oct)
1. Power-cycle through EN: scope or probe IO1, which must not go high at power-up; or read the counter's first `Occ: state=0 ... session=` after power-up. About 570 s means no glitch; about 576 s means a rising edge at power-up (6 s quiet period added). The first session on Dev-14 read 581 s, which a 6 s glitch alone does not explain, so about 5 s is unaccounted for (wake latency is the likely part).
2. One full cycle: green, blue pulses 2 to 10, 5 minutes of trailing blue blinks, red heartbeat.
